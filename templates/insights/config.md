## Focus Areas

Which pillars and collections to monitor for external signals.

| Area | Enabled | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Space | Yes | Competitive landscape shifts, new entrants, positioning changes |
| Audience | Yes | Role evolution, new buyer personas, shifting priorities |
| Portfolio | Yes | Feature parity, new categories, technology shifts |
| Proof | Yes | New customer stories, analyst mentions, review site trends |
| Motion | No | GTM channel changes (enable when motion is stable) |

## Alert Thresholds

What triggers a new insight in the tracker.

| Signal | Threshold | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor positioning change | Any | New tagline, messaging pivot, or category claim |
| New competitor | Any | Previously untracked company entering the space |
| Analyst mention | Any | Report, blog, or social mention by industry analyst |
| Review site trend | 3+ mentions | Recurring theme in customer reviews (positive or negative) |
| Role/title shift | Pattern | New job titles or role definitions appearing in target accounts |
