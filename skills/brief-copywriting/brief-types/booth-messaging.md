# Booth Messaging

Physical-space messaging for event booths, demo stations, and meeting rooms — designed to be consumed standing up in 10-second increments. Every element must work at scan speed.

## Structure

| Section | Purpose | Guidance |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Banner Headline | Stop traffic in 5-7 words | Name the outcome or tension, not the product category |
| Tagline | Expand the headline (one sentence) | Connect the headline to the audience's world |
| Key Messages (3) | Core value propositions, one sentence each | Each must stand alone — visitors see them out of order |
| Conversation Starters | Open dialogue with booth visitors (5 questions) | Qualifying questions that surface pain, not product interest |
| Demo Flow | Guided walkthrough sequence (3-5 steps) | Problem → solution → outcome arc in under 5 minutes |
| Qualifying Questions | Identify fit during conversation (5 questions) | Separate curiosity from intent; surface timeline and authority |
| Competitive Redirects | Handle "how are you different from X?" (3-5 entries) | Reframe to your strength, don't disparage the competitor |

## Tone & Style

- **Voice:** Confident, direct, conversational — not formal or corporate
- **Length:** Banner headline 5-7 words; tagline under 15 words; key messages one sentence each; total document 400-600 words
- **Altitude:** Accessible to anyone walking the floor — no assumed product familiarity

Booth messaging is environmental, not editorial. People scan banners while walking past. They read key messages while waiting for a demo. They hear conversation starters in noisy halls. Every element must work at the speed of a passing glance or a 30-second conversation.

## Content Guidelines

- **Banner headline:** Test by imagining it on a 10-foot banner — does it work at 20 feet away?
- **Key messages:** Each should trigger a head nod from the target persona — "yes, that's my problem"
- **Conversation starters:** Open-ended questions, not yes/no — "What does your team do when they find an asset that's not in any tool?" not "Do you have asset visibility challenges?"
- **Demo flow:** Start with the problem the visitor just described, not a product tour. End with a metric or outcome.
- **Qualifying questions:** Natural in conversation, not interrogative — weave into dialogue
- **Competitive redirects:** Acknowledge the competitor, pivot to a differentiator, offer proof — never dismiss or disparage

## Example

**Input:** Create booth messaging for a security conference booth focused on attack surface management

**Output:**
```markdown
## Brief Specification
**Type:** Booth Messaging
**Target Audience:** Security practitioners and leaders at RSA Conference
**Objective:** Drive booth engagement and qualified demo conversations
**Key Message:** Continuous visibility into every asset eliminates the blind spots attackers exploit
**Proof Points:** 15% visibility gap statistic, Fortune 500 discovery example, 12,000 unknown assets

## Content Outline
Banner Headline → Tagline → Key Messages → Conversation Starters → Demo Flow → Qualifying Questions → Competitive Redirects

## Brief Content

### Banner Headline

**See Every Asset. Secure What Matters.**

### Tagline

You're missing 15% of your assets. We can show you which ones.

### Key Messages

1. **Complete visibility** — Aggregate asset data from every tool in your stack into a single, continuously updated inventory.
2. **Coverage gap detection** — Know which assets are missing security controls before auditors or attackers find them.
3. **Automated response** — Trigger remediation workflows the moment an asset drifts out of policy.

### Conversation Starters

1. "What's your process when an auditor asks how many assets you have — and you're not sure?"
2. "When you onboard a new security tool, how do you know it's covering every asset it should?"
3. "What happens in your org when someone spins up a cloud instance that doesn't show up in your CMDB?"
4. "How does your team prioritize which vulnerability findings to fix first?"
5. "What's the gap between what your asset inventory says and what's actually on your network?"

### Demo Flow

1. **Start with their world** — "Let me show you what it looks like when we connect to the tools you already have." Pull up the aggregation dashboard.
2. **Surface the gap** — "Here's what one customer found: 12,000 assets that weren't in any other tool." Show the discovery view.
3. **Show coverage analysis** — "Now for each of those assets, here's which security tools are covering it — and which aren't." Display the coverage gap matrix.
4. **Demonstrate response** — "When an asset falls out of policy, this is what happens automatically." Trigger a remediation workflow.
5. **Land the metric** — "This customer went from 85% to 99% asset coverage in 90 days."

### Qualifying Questions

1. "How many assets does your security team manage across cloud and on-prem?"
2. "Who in your org is responsible for maintaining the asset inventory?"
3. "Are you currently under any compliance mandates that require full asset accounting?"
4. "What's your timeline for addressing visibility gaps — is this a current initiative?"
5. "Who else would need to be involved in evaluating a solution like this?"

### Competitive Redirects

**"How are you different from [CMDB vendor]?"**
"CMDBs are great for IT operations, but they rely on what gets entered. We aggregate from 400+ sources automatically — so you see what's actually there, not just what someone documented."

**"We already use [vulnerability scanner]."**
"That's a key data source for us. We don't replace it — we show you which assets it's covering and which it's missing. Most customers find a 10-20% gap."

**"What about [cloud security posture tool]?"**
"Cloud posture tools cover cloud. We cover everything — cloud, on-prem, OT, IoT, SaaS. The full picture, not one slice."

## Design Notes
- Banner headline and tagline sized for 10-foot booth backdrop
- Key messages on standing banners or monitor displays flanking the demo station
- Conversation starters on a pocket card for booth staff
- Qualifying questions integrated into booth staff training, not displayed publicly
```

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Target | Red Flags |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Headline Impact | Stops foot traffic; works at 20 feet on a banner | Reads like a tagline, not a headline; too long or too vague |
| Message Scannability | Each key message understood in under 5 seconds | Dense sentences, compound ideas, or jargon |
| Conversation Utility | Starters surface real pain and open dialogue | Yes/no questions, leading questions, or product-first framing |
| Demo Flow | Problem-to-outcome arc in under 5 minutes | Product tour without problem framing; no customer proof |
| Qualifying Depth | Questions separate curiosity from buying intent | Only surface-level questions; no timeline or authority signals |
| Competitive Readiness | Redirects acknowledge, pivot, and prove — without disparaging | Dismissing competitors or making unsubstantiated claims |
