# Product Collateral

PDF leave-behind covering product overview, capabilities, customer evidence, and pricing. Used in sales meetings and follow-ups.

## Dependencies

- messaging/profile.md
- messaging/portfolio.md
- messaging/proof.md

## Trigger Conditions

- Product capabilities or pricing changes
- New customer proof points added
- Insight severity: critical (immediate), warning (next scheduled review)
- Scheduled: quarterly

## Structure

| Section | Source |
|---|---|
| Product overview | profile.md, portfolio.md |
| Capabilities | portfolio.md |
| Customer evidence | proof.md |
| Pricing | portfolio.md |
