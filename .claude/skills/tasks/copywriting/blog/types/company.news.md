# Company News

Announce company milestones, partnerships, executive hires, funding, awards, or strategic updates in a way that reinforces positioning and generates earned media pickup.

## Structure

| Section | Purpose | Guidance |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Headline | Lead with what changed and why it matters | News-first, not company-first. "X Achieves Y" beats "[Company] Announces Z" |
| Lead | The essential facts in 2-3 sentences | Who, what, when, why it matters. A journalist should be able to write their lede from yours |
| Context | Why this matters to the market | Connect the news to a market trend, customer need, or strategic direction. This is where positioning lives |
| Details | The specifics | What happened, the numbers, the partners, the scope. Be concrete |
| Proof | Validation from others | Customer quote, partner quote, analyst context. Third-party validation carries the credibility |
| Looking Ahead | What this enables next | Forward-looking without overpromising. Connect to the company's strategic narrative |
| Boilerplate | Company description | Pull directly from profile.md boilerplate statement |

## Tone & Style

- **Voice:** Confident, factual, measured. Newsworthy without being breathless
- **Length:** 600-1,000 words
- **Altitude:** Executive-accessible, journalist-friendly

Write for two audiences simultaneously: the prospect who sees this on your blog and the journalist who might cover it. Both need facts first, context second, spin never.

## SEO/GEO Optimization

- Headline should work as-is for search results and social sharing
- Include the company name and key terms in the first paragraph
- Structured data-friendly: clear who/what/when/where
- Include a quotable executive statement for AI engines to extract

## Example

**Input:** Write a company news post about a $50M Series C funding round

**Output:**
```markdown
# [Company] Raises $50M to Expand Exposure Management Platform

[Company] today announced a $50 million Series C round led by [Investor],
bringing total funding to $85 million. The investment will accelerate
platform development and international expansion.

## Why It Matters

The funding arrives as enterprises increasingly consolidate security tooling...

[Continues with Details → Proof → Looking Ahead → Boilerplate]
```

## Quality Signals

| Criterion | Target | Red Flags |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Newsworthiness | Clear "what changed" in the first two sentences | Burying the news below company background |
| Market Connection | News linked to a trend or customer need | Self-congratulatory tone with no external relevance |
| Quotability | At least one executive quote and one third-party quote | No external validation, only company voice |
| Positioning Discipline | News reinforces existing positioning without overreaching | Using the announcement to introduce new positioning claims |
| Journalist Readiness | A reporter could write a story from this without additional research | Missing key facts, vague numbers, no named sources |
