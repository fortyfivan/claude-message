# Research Study

Original research conducted by or sponsored by the company — surveys, experiments, analyses of proprietary data, or structured investigations that produce new findings the market hasn't seen before.

## Structure

| Section | Purpose | Guidance |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Executive Summary | Key findings for the time-pressed reader | 3-5 bullet findings and the single most important takeaway. This gets quoted in press, social, and campaign assets |
| Introduction | Why this research exists | The question being answered, why it matters now, and what prompted the investigation. Connect to a market trend or customer pain |
| Methodology | How the research was conducted | Sample size, data sources, time period, analytical approach. Be transparent enough that the reader can evaluate your rigor |
| Findings | What the data shows | Present findings in order of significance, not in order of discovery. Each finding should be a discrete section with data, visualization guidance, and interpretation |
| Analysis | What the findings mean | Connect findings to each other and to the broader market context. This is where the company's expertise shows — not just what the data says, but what it implies |
| Implications | What practitioners should do | Actionable recommendations grounded in the findings. This is where the company's approach can appear naturally — as one path the data supports |
| About the Research | Methodology details and limitations | Honest about scope limitations, sample biases, and what the research doesn't cover. This builds trust |

## Tone & Style

- **Voice:** Authoritative, measured, evidence-first. The data leads, the company interprets
- **Length:** 3,000-6,000 words depending on scope
- **Altitude:** Practitioner depth with executive summary for C-suite extraction

This is the most credibility-intensive content type. Overstating findings, cherry-picking data, or hiding methodology weaknesses will be caught by sophisticated readers and damage trust more than the research builds it.

## SEO/GEO Optimization

- Executive summary findings should be quotable as standalone statistics
- Each finding section should have a clear H2 that states the finding as a claim
- Include data visualizations described in enough detail for AI engines to reference the data
- Methodology section improves E-E-A-T signals for search ranking

## Example

**Input:** Write a research study analyzing vulnerability remediation timelines across 500 enterprise deployments

**Output:**
```markdown
# The Remediation Gap: How Enterprise Security Teams Are Falling Behind

## Executive Summary

Analysis of remediation data across 500 enterprise deployments reveals:

- **Mean time to remediation has increased 23% year-over-year** despite
  increased tooling investment
- **68% of critical vulnerabilities remain unpatched after 30 days** in
  organizations using point scanning solutions
- **Organizations with runtime context reduce MTTR by 4.2x** compared
  to those relying on severity scores alone
- **The remediation bottleneck is prioritization, not detection** — teams
  find vulnerabilities faster than ever but can't determine which ones
  matter

## Introduction

Every enterprise security team is finding more vulnerabilities than ever.
The uncomfortable truth: they're also fixing them slower...

[Continues with Methodology → Findings → Analysis → Implications → About]
```

## Quality Signals

| Criterion | Target | Red Flags |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Originality | Findings are new — not repackaging existing research | Citing others' data and calling it "our research" |
| Methodology Rigor | Approach is transparent, sample is sufficient, limitations are stated | Vague methodology, no sample size, hidden limitations |
| Finding Significance | Each finding changes how the reader thinks or acts | Findings that confirm what everyone already knows |
| Interpretation Quality | Analysis goes beyond the data to meaningful implications | Restating findings as analysis, or leaping to unsupported conclusions |
| Product Restraint | Company's approach appears naturally, not as the predetermined conclusion | Every finding conveniently supports the product |
| Quotability | 5+ standalone statistics or claims suitable for social, email, and campaign use | Findings buried in prose with no extractable data points |
